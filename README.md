# MultiMind — Your Personality, Mapped

**Journals that synthesize your inner world.**

MultiMind is a multi-journal system that synthesizes your thoughts, dreams, and relationships into a comprehensive, evolving map of your personality and mental landscape. Multiple journal types capture every dimension of your inner world — each entry is analyzed and connected, building a living portrait of who you are.

## Features
- Multi-format journals for thoughts, dreams, relationships, and more
- AI-powered extraction of themes, personas, and emotional patterns
- Interactive mind map showing connections between your experiences
- Personality synthesis that evolves with every entry
- Mood tracking and sentiment analysis
- Privacy-first — your data stays yours
